# Safety and Contingency Plan

## Mandatory GGGW rules

- All dogs remain on leads at all times.
- Dogs are muzzled when required.
- Owners clean up after their dogs.
- Participants respect other park users.
- The walk leader communicates rules before departure.
- Owners remain responsible for themselves and their dogs.

## Local event rules

- No retractable leashes.
- One capable handler per dog whenever practical.
- No forced dog greetings.
- Yellow ribbon or visible marker may identify dogs needing space.
- Dogs and handlers stay on designated paths.
- No off-leash activity, including during the picnic.
- Dogs remain out of playgrounds and organized athletic fields.
- Use individual water containers, not communal bowls.

## Weather decision framework

Use current National Weather Service information and veterinary guidance; do not rely on a single generic temperature threshold.

- **Normal:** Walk and picnic proceed.
- **Marginal heat/humidity:** Short route only; extend shaded rest; optional walk.
- **Unsafe heat or pavement:** Cancel walk; hold only a brief shaded meetup if venue and GPA Houston agree it is safe.
- **Lightning, severe thunderstorm, flooding or dangerous wind:** Cancel the entire event.

Publish the event-morning decision by 6:00 a.m. The safety lead retains authority to stop activity after that decision.

## Signs of canine heat illness

Excessive panting, drooling, weakness, confusion, vomiting, abnormal gum color or collapse require immediate cooling and veterinary attention. Do not force an overheated dog to drink large quantities or use ice-cold immersion without veterinary direction.

## Picnic hazards

Keep dogs away from chocolate, xylitol, grapes/raisins, onions/garlic, alcohol, cooked bones, skewers, toothpicks, wrappers and high-fat foods. Food and waste remain secured above ground.

## Incident response

1. Stop activity and protect the scene.
2. Call 911 for human life threats; contact an emergency veterinarian for animal emergencies.
3. Assign one person to meet responders at the park entrance.
4. Move other dogs and attendees away.
5. Record factual details, witnesses and actions without assigning blame.
6. Notify the event lead, GPA Houston contact and venue as required.
7. Preserve relevant registration/waiver records.

## Loose-dog protocol

- Do not chase or shout.
- Announce the loose dog calmly and stop group movement.
- Secure all other dogs and food.
- Designate one familiar handler to approach with approved high-value food if safe.
- Place volunteers at vehicle exits without entering traffic.
- Call park authorities or emergency services when public safety is threatened.

## Required safety supplies

- Human first-aid kit
- Canine first-aid kit

- Cooling towels
- Waste bags and trash bags
- Charged phones and power bank
- Printed emergency contacts and route map
- Incident forms, pen and clipboard
