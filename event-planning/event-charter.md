# Event Charter

## Purpose

Celebrate sighthounds, promote responsible ownership and adoption, and connect the Houston sighthound community through the 2026 Great Global Greyhound Walk.

## Objectives

- Conduct a safe, welcoming walk at Memorial Park on September 27, 2026.
- Limit attendance to no more than 50 people, including volunteers.
- Provide one combined check-in and Greyhound Pets of America Houston information station.
- Offer an optional bring-your-own picnic after the walk.
- Accurately report the total number of participating sighthounds to GGGW before 6:00 p.m. local time.
- Leave the reserved area and route clean and undamaged.

## Scope

### Included

- Advance registration and waitlist
- Reserved pavilion or picnic area
- Approximately 0.75-mile designated-path walk, with a shorter turnaround
- Required pre-walk safety briefing
- Group photograph
- Household BYO picnic
- GPA Houston adoption, foster and volunteer information
- Weather monitoring and incident response

### Excluded

- Race, 5K or timed activity
- Street or lane closure
- More than one booth or station
- Outside vendors
- Organizer-provided or shared food
- Alcohol, cooking, grills, propane or open flames
- Amplified music or entertainment
- Off-leash activity
- Merchandise sales unless specifically approved

## Participants

All eligible sighthounds and sighthound mixes may participate under GGGW rules. Each dog must have a capable handler. Picnic participation is optional.

## Success measures

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Safety | No loose dogs, heat injuries or preventable incidents |
| Capacity | 50 people or fewer, including volunteers |
| Schedule | Walk begins within 10 minutes of plan |
| Compliance | Written venue approval and all required insurance/permits secured |
| Reporting | One reconciled sighthound total submitted before 6:00 p.m. |
| Stewardship | All waste and event materials removed |
| Outreach | GPA Houston adoption/foster/volunteer inquiries recorded |

## Constraints and assumptions

- Memorial Park approval is not yet documented.
- Reservations are generally made in three-hour blocks and should be submitted at least 10 business days ahead.
- Houston may remain hot and humid in late September.
- The exact facility, route, fees and insurance requirements remain pending.
- GGGW requires dogs to remain leashed and owners to remain responsible for their dogs.
