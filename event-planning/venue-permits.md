# Venue, Reservation and Approval Plan

## Proposed venue

**Memorial Park, Houston** — preferred area: a Picnic Loop covered pavilion or shaded picnic area. Northside picnic areas are the secondary option. Clay Family Eastern Glades is deprioritized because a scheduled exhibit runs through September 27, 2026.

## Known reservation information

- Picnic facilities are available at Eastern Glades, Picnic Loop and Northside picnic areas.
- Facilities are reserved in three-hour blocks, first come-first served.
- Memorial Park requests at least 10 business days' notice.
- Applications are generally reviewed within five business days.
- The Conservancy states that capacity for 5K and fun-run events is extremely limited.

## Accurate event description

> Greyhound Pets of America Houston proposes a pre-registered Great Global Greyhound Walk on Sunday, September 27, 2026. Attendance is capped at 50 people, including volunteers. This is a relaxed, noncompetitive social walk on existing designated paths—not a race or 5K. All dogs remain leashed. The event concludes with an optional bring-your-own picnic. Each household brings and consumes its own food and nonalcoholic drinks; organizers do not prepare, sell, distribute or serve food. There will be one combined check-in/GPA Houston information station and no vendors, amplified sound, street closures, cooking, grills, propane, open flame or alcohol.
