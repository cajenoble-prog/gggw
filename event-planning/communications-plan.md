## Public event description

**Great Global Greyhound Walk — Houston**  
Join Greyhound Pets of America Houston for a relaxed, noncompetitive leashed sighthound walk at Memorial Park, followed by an optional bring-your-own picnic. Advance registration is required, and total attendance is capped at 50 people. Exact meeting details will be sent to confirmed registrants.

## Confirmation message

Subject: Confirmed — Houston Great Global Greyhound Walk

Thank you for registering. Your party is included in the 50-person event capacity. The event includes a short leashed walk followed by an optional bring-your-own picnic. Each household should bring food and nonalcoholic drinks only for itself; organizers will not provide or distribute food.

Please bring a fixed-length leash, dog water and bowl, waste bags, weather protection, and a muzzle if your dog normally requires one. Do not bring alcohol, glass, grills, open flames, shared food or retractable leashes. Keep food secured and give all dogs adequate space.

## Event-morning message templates

### Proceeding

The Houston GGGW is proceeding as planned. Check-in opens at 8:00 a.m. at the confirmed Memorial Park picnic area. Conditions will be monitored, and participants may choose the shorter route or picnic only.

### Modified

Due to heat or trail conditions, today's walk will use the short route. The shaded bring-your-own picnic will continue as approved. Participation in the walk is optional.

### Cancelled

Today's Houston GGGW is cancelled because conditions are unsafe. Please do not travel to the park. Information about any follow-up gathering will be sent separately.

## Mandatory verbal briefing

- Welcome and identify GPA Houston affiliation.
- Confirm that the activity is noncompetitive and paths remain open to the public.
- All dogs remain leashed; no retractable leashes or forced greetings.
- Muzzle dogs when required and give yellow-ribbon dogs additional room.
- Keep right, yield to other users and follow marshals.
- Point out short turnaround and permission to leave at any time.
- Explain heat symptoms and emergency procedure.
- During the picnic, secure food and keep dogs away from other households.
- Clean up all dog and picnic waste.

## Post-event message

Thank attendees and volunteers, report the final sighthound count after GGGW confirmation, share only approved photographs, provide GPA Houston adoption/foster links, and invite concise feedback.
