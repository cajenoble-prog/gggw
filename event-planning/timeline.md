# Timeline and Milestones

Event date: **Sunday, September 27, 2026**

## August 29–September 1: Initiation

- [ ] Obtain written GPA Houston authorization and name the organizational contact.
- [ ] Assign event lead, safety lead and registration lead.
- [ ] Contact Memorial Park Conservancy at `events@memorialparkconservancy.org` or 713-863-8403.
- [ ] Submit a pavilion/picnic-area reservation request.
- [ ] Disclose the walk, dogs, one information station and BYO picnic.
- [ ] Ask about insurance, waiver, permit, signage and equipment requirements.
- [ ] Establish preliminary budget ceiling.

**Milestone:** Venue approval path established.

## September 2–4: Venue and route

- [ ] Tour candidate Picnic Loop or Northside facilities.
- [ ] Scout the route on a Sunday-morning-equivalent schedule if possible.
- [ ] Record parking, shade, restrooms, water, accessibility and trail conflicts.
- [ ] Select full and short-turnaround routes.
- [ ] Confirm event calendar conflicts and construction closures.
- [ ] Update the risk assessment.

**September 4 gate:** Continue only if a viable facility and approval route exist.

## September 5–8: Launch preparation

- [ ] Obtain final venue/reservation details in writing.
- [ ] Secure insurance or waivers if required.
- [ ] Register the walk with GGGW.
- [ ] Build registration form with hard capacity and waitlist.
- [ ] Obtain approval for GPA Houston branding.
- [ ] Recruit six volunteers.
- [ ] Prepare route map, parking pin and event description.

**Milestone:** Event ready for controlled publication.

## September 9–13: Registration launch

- [ ] Open registration for up to 45 non-volunteer attendees.
- [ ] Publish leash, picnic, heat and photography rules.
- [ ] Confirm volunteer assignments.
- [ ] Identify nearest open emergency veterinary hospital.
- [ ] Inventory available GPA Houston equipment.
- [ ] Order or purchase missing supplies.

**September 11 gate:** Written venue direction, insurance path and route must be clear.

## September 14–18: Operational readiness

- [ ] Review attendee and dog counts daily.
- [ ] Send confirmations and move waitlisted guests into cancellations.
- [ ] Finalize safety briefing and lost-dog procedure.
- [ ] Confirm tables, seating, signage and trash requirements.
- [ ] Verify restrooms and accessible parking.
- [ ] Prepare printed check-in and emergency sheets.
- [ ] Conduct team readiness review.

**September 18 gate:** Proceed only with adequate volunteers and safety resources.

## September 19–23: Final coordination

- [ ] Reconfirm reservation and approved setup with Memorial Park.
- [ ] Recheck park calendar, alerts and trail status.
- [ ] Send attendee logistics message with exact map pin.
- [ ] Close general registration at capacity.
- [ ] Reconcile people count, dog count and volunteer count.
- [ ] Pack and label equipment by function.

## September 24–26: Final checks

- [ ] Monitor forecast and heat index.
- [ ] Confirm every volunteer by phone or message.
- [ ] Print final rosters and signs.
- [ ] Charge phones and power banks.
- [ ] Send final reminder and weather policy.
- [ ] Make go/no-go decision by September 25 review; retain event-morning safety authority.

## September 27: Event day

- [ ] Conduct pre-opening route and pavement inspection.
- [ ] Check in attendees and count sighthounds.
- [ ] Deliver mandatory rules briefing.
- [ ] Conduct walk and picnic according to run of show.
- [ ] Reconcile final dog count.
- [ ] Remove all waste and materials.
- [ ] Submit total sighthound attendance to GGGW before 6:00 p.m.

## September 28–October 2: Closeout

- [ ] Thank attendees, volunteers and Memorial Park staff.
- [ ] Reconcile actual expenses and receipts.
- [ ] Record incidents, feedback and GPA Houston inquiries.
- [ ] Share approved photographs.
- [ ] Send any required report to GPA Houston or venue.
- [ ] Document lessons learned for 2027.
