# Agent Guidance

## Project purpose

This workspace manages the 2026 Houston Great Global Greyhound Walk, affiliated with Greyhound Pets of America Houston.

## Confirmed event parameters

- Date: Sunday, September 27, 2026
- Venue: Memorial Park, Houston; exact picnic facility pending written confirmation
- Capacity: 50 people maximum, including volunteers
- Format: short, noncompetitive leashed sighthound walk followed by an optional bring-your-own picnic
- Event footprint: one combined check-in and GPA Houston information station
- Food model: each household brings and consumes its own food; organizers do not provide or distribute food
- GGGW theme: Mythical Mutts

## Working practices

1. Preserve the confirmed event parameters unless the user explicitly changes them.
2. Treat every item marked **Pending confirmation** as unapproved.
3. Do not invent permits, fees, policies, contacts, route details, or venue approval.
4. Prefer current official GGGW, Memorial Park Conservancy, GPA Houston, and City of Houston sources.
5. Include source URLs in `webpages.md` when new external facts affect planning.
6. Record material decisions and official responses in `event-planning/contacts-decisions.md`.
7. Add or update actionable work in `event-planning/action-tracker.md` with one owner, due date, status, and dependency.
8. Keep dates and capacity figures consistent across all planning documents.
9. Use concise Markdown tables and checklists that organizers can maintain manually.
10. Link related documents using relative workspace paths.

## Safety and compliance constraints

- All dogs must remain leashed; no off-leash activity is in scope.
- Do not plan a race, timed event, 5K, street closure, outside vendor, amplified entertainment, cooking, grill, propane, open flame, or alcohol.
- Do not describe the event as approved until written approval is recorded.
- Keep the total onsite population at or below 50, including volunteers.
- Track people and sighthounds separately.
- Maintain short-route and weather-modification options.
- Keep the picnic household-based rather than a potluck or shared buffet.
- Avoid definitive medical or legal claims; identify when professional or official confirmation is needed.

## Privacy and data handling

- Collect only information necessary for registration, accessibility, emergency communication, and attendance reporting.
- Do not add sensitive medical details, payment data, private addresses, or unnecessary personal information to the repository.
- Prefer organizational contact details over personal details.
- Keep examples and templates populated with placeholders rather than real attendee data.

## Editing expectations

- Update existing documents instead of creating duplicates.
- When one decision affects multiple documents, update every affected source-of-truth file in the same task.
- Preserve completed decisions in the decision log; supersede them with a new dated entry rather than silently rewriting history.
- Validate internal links, dates, totals, statuses, and terminology after broad changes.
- Do not modify or remove source images unless explicitly requested.

## Completion criteria

A planning change is complete when:

- the relevant plan is updated;
- resulting tasks, owners, and deadlines are reflected in the action tracker;
- any material decision is logged;
- pending official confirmation is clearly identified;
- affected dates, capacity counts, and event descriptions remain consistent; and
- no private attendee information has been introduced.
